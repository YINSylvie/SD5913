# My Process

## How I Used AI

I used ChatGPT for brainstorming, organizing the structure of my essay, and checking and revising my English so that the grammar and wording were more accurate.

However, the central argument of the essay and my personal project experiences were not created by AI. They came from my own thoughts and experiences as an animation student working with games, VR, and digital media.

## Something I Kept

I kept the distinction between “describing” and “specifying” that ChatGPT helped me develop during the revision process.

My initial idea was to explain how giving AI an emotional adjective, such as “unsettling,” is different from specifying how that feeling should be created. ChatGPT helped me explain this through more concrete examples, including changes in space, delayed sounds, unusual object movements, and the relationship between a digital space and the player's memories.

I kept this part because it helped me clarify an abstract concept that was difficult to explain at first. It also brought up aspects I had not considered carefully enough and connected the idea to my own creative practice. More importantly, it strengthened my argument: the real challenge in the age of AI is not simply whether AI can write code, but whether an artist can express their artistic intention clearly enough to turn it into a working experience.

## Something I Rejected

I rejected some conclusions generated by AI that suggested artists would always be more creative than AI.

Although this idea sounded positive and encouraging, it was too general and absolute. It did not really express my argument, and it was not something I could confidently claim.

Instead, I chose to focus on the value of understanding a creative medium. My final argument is not that artists are necessarily more creative than machines, but that artists still need to understand, evaluate, and decide how to use the tools they depend on.

I do not know how much programming will change during my career in the age of AI, but I believe that the ability to keep learning new systems and rules, and to evaluate the results they produce, will remain important.

## Reflection

AI helped me work more efficiently. It could quickly help me find relevant information, overcome language barriers, and translate my ideas from Chinese into English more accurately. It also helped me identify problems in my argument.

However, I also noticed that AI can make mistakes. It often produces content that sounds reasonable but may turn out to be repetitive, meaningless, or different from what I actually intended to say.

Therefore, I think using AI appropriately can be very helpful in my creative work. But I still need to decide what the core ideas are, organize my thoughts, and make the final judgments myself.
